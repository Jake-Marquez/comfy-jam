# Game ideas
 - Leaf blower simulator: your goal is to keep the sidewalk clear for trick or treaters, but every time you look away from a tree it drops more leaves.
 - Black cat simulator: you run a coffee shop and give customers orders but your shop cat has bad luck and knocks over boxes unless you keep an eye on it and give catnip or spray with water.
 - Offensive pumpkins: you grow a pumpkin patch, but evil gnomes sneak in at night to carve offensive shapes on them so you keep watch with a flashlight to scare them off.
